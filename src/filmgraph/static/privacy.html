PILOT NOTICE · 22 OCTOBER 2026 · DRAFT

Privacy and your choices

This notice describes the current pilot. Operator identity, a working privacy contact, retention procedures and legal review must be completed before a public commercial launch. This pilot is intended for adults aged 18 and older.

What is stored

Guest favourites, watchlists and watched films are saved in this browser. A signed-in account stores your email through Supabase Auth, your saved films, optional ratings, followed people/films/companies, preferences and in-app notifications. Passwords are handled by Supabase Auth, not stored in the film catalogue. Session cookies keep you signed in.

Queries and limits

The server receives your search to retrieve results. The application does not intentionally save raw chat text or sell query history. Daily query limits use a keyed hash of the guest IP address, or an account ID, and query fingerprints. Hosting, authentication and network providers may retain operational logs according to their policies. Guest IP addresses are processed transiently to calculate a quota identity; a hash is not a guarantee of anonymisation.

Recommendations and analytics

Personalisation is off by default. If you enable it, selected saved-film IDs are sent to our server to calculate recommendations from shared credits. You can turn it off without losing search or your lists. Optional analytics consent is stored separately; this pilot has no advertising tracker and does not currently collect analytics events. We do not sell your personal data, share your watchlist with advertisers, or use it to train a model.

Alerts

Following a person, film or production company records that subscription. News feeds and email/push delivery are not connected in the pilot. Enabling a follow does not subscribe you to marketing email. Delivery will require a separate choice when it becomes available.

External links and processors

Supabase provides account and database services; Vercel provides hosting when the hosted version is used. Clicking Wikipedia, IMDb, YouTube, JustWatch or an OTT provider takes you to that service under its own terms. Current outbound links are ordinary search or source links, with no affiliate commission configured. If commercial affiliate links are added, they must be disclosed beside the link.

Control and removal

The My collection panel lets you disable personalisation, export your collection, clear locally stored data, and remove saved items. Signed-in users can request account deletion in that panel; deleting an account removes its lists and preferences through database ownership rules. Infrastructure backups and provider security logs may remain until their configured expiry. Quota fingerprints need a scheduled deletion job; the hosted retention job must be verified before launch.

Browser model experiment

The optional model lab downloads model files only after you choose Load model. Inference runs on your device. Model/CDN hosts can observe the file download. The model lab does not execute its generated plan; the normal query API independently validates entities and permitted templates.

Before public launch, publish a real operator/privacy contact, specific retention periods, rights and grievance procedures, international transfer details where applicable, and reviewed notices in the languages the service supports.
