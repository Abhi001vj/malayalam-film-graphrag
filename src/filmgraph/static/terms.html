PILOT TERMS · 22 OCTOBER 2026 · DRAFT

Using FilmGraph

FilmGraph is an experimental cinema research and discovery service for adults aged 18 and older. These draft pilot terms need operator/contact details and legal review before commercial launch.

Source-based results

Credits, collaboration counts, story matches and recommendations reflect imported source claims. They may be incomplete, inconsistent or incorrect. Verify important facts through the linked sources. Technical mentions may refer to a re-release or a comparison. Film catalogue dates do not establish current theatrical or OTT availability.

Accounts and query limits

Guests (per network address) may ask 5 questions per minute and 20 distinct questions per UTC day; signed-in users 20 per minute and 200 per day. Repeating the same question does not use another daily question, but every request counts toward the per-minute limit. A paid tier without the daily cap may be offered later. Filters can make a new query. Limits can change with notice. Guest quotas are best-effort and may be shared by users on one network. Do not automate abuse, bypass limits, or interfere with the service.

Your collection

You retain control of your private lists and preferences. We process them to provide the features you request. Personalisation and optional analytics have separate choices. Search remains available when personalisation is disabled. See the privacy notice for data practices and removal controls.

Third-party content and links

Wikipedia-derived text is attributed through page/revision links under its stated licence. Wikidata structured data is CC0. IMDb's noncommercial datasets are included only in the local personal research database; the default deployment export removes imported IMDb dataset claims. IMDb identifiers and external links from Wikimedia may remain. Letterboxd data is not scraped. No licence to stream, download, redistribute or commercially exploit third-party films or data is granted by this service.

Recommendations and revenue

Recommendations are suggestions, not guarantees. There are no paid subscriptions, affiliate commissions or sponsored rankings configured in this pilot. Any future paid tier, sponsorship or affiliate link requires separate, clear pricing/disclosure and applicable data rights. Our current policy is not to sell personal viewing data.

Pilot availability

The pilot is provided for evaluation and may change or become unavailable. These terms do not limit mandatory rights under applicable law. Production terms must identify the operator, jurisdiction, support and grievance contact and other required details before launch.
